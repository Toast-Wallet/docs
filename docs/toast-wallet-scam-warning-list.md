This page is the single reference list for every known Toast Wallet-related scam. Bookmark it,
and share it with anyone you know who used Toast Wallet between 2017 and 2020.

**1. The "Toast Plus" fraudulent app.** Listed on the Apple App Store and Google Play
(`co.peninsulasoftware.toastwallet`), using a name and logo close enough to fool searchers. Has
stolen an estimated $5M+ in XRP and was the subject of a 2021 federal class action against Apple
(*Diep v. Apple Inc.*). Full details: [The Toast Plus App Scam](./toast-plus-app-scam.md).

**2. The toastwallet.app phishing site.** A clone of the official recovery page that drains any
wallet imported into it. Linked by infrastructure to the same operator as "Toast Plus" and
"Droplet Wallet." Full details: [Toast Wallet Phishing Sites](./toast-wallet-phishing-sites.md).

**3. SEO marketing clone sites.** `xrp-toastwallet.com`, `toast-wallet.com`, `en-toastwallet.com`,
`toast-wallet.net` — not official, describe Toast Wallet inaccurately, one flagged with a low
trust score by independent checkers.

**4. Tracked scammer-linked XRP addresses.** Multiple independent victims have reported their
stolen funds routed to the same small set of destination addresses, including
`r3XGwhMrnfWf72vm3Yh5H23PiLoDKxcXc` (reported by one victim to hold over 2 million XRP at one
point). If you see a transaction to an address like this in your wallet history that you didn't
authorize, you were very likely a victim of one of the scams above — not a "bug" in the real Toast
Wallet software.

**5. Recovery-scam follow-up fraud.** This one catches people *after* they've already been
scammed. If you post publicly (Reddit, X, forums) asking for help recovering stolen XRP, expect to
be contacted by someone offering to "recover" it for an upfront fee, or claiming to have hacking
tools that can reverse the theft. **This is always a second scam.** No XRP Ledger transaction can
be reversed by anyone, for any fee. Never pay anyone claiming to offer this.

**What to do if you were affected:**
1. Stop using the fraudulent app or site immediately.
2. Check your wallet address on a public explorer (bithomp.com or xrpscan.com) to confirm what
   happened to your funds.
3. File a report at **ic3.gov** — this matters for any future law enforcement action, even though
   individual recovery usually isn't possible.
4. Ignore any DM or reply offering paid recovery help.
5. If you still have a legitimate old Toast Wallet with funds in it, follow our
   [official recovery guide](./how-to-recover-xrp-toast-wallet.md) using **only** the links in
   the box below.

## ⚠️ Known Scams — Do Not Use These

- **"Toast Plus"** — a fraudulent lookalike app that has stolen an estimated **$5M+ in XRP** and was the subject of a 2021 federal lawsuit against Apple. Read: [The Toast Plus App Scam](./toast-plus-app-scam.md).
- **toastwallet.app** — a phishing clone of the official recovery page; anything imported here gets stolen within minutes. Read: [Toast Wallet Phishing Sites](./toast-wallet-phishing-sites.md).
- **Clone marketing sites** (`xrp-toastwallet.com`, `toast-wallet.com`, `en-toastwallet.com`, `toast-wallet.net`) — unofficial, with fabricated claims about the product.
- **Anyone offering paid XRP "recovery" after you post about a loss** — this is always fraud.

Full reference: [Toast Wallet Scam Alert: Full Warning List](./toast-wallet-scam-warning-list.md). Report scams at [ic3.gov](https://www.ic3.gov).
