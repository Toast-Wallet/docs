Because Toast Wallet is discontinued, "support" and "account deletion" work differently than
you'd expect from an actively maintained product — there's no customer service team to contact.

**There is no official customer support today.** StarStone Ltd stepped back from active
development in 2020. There is no live chat, support email, or ticketing system monitoring the
official channels. This is also exactly why scammers offering "Toast Wallet support" or "account
recovery services" over DM or email should be treated as an automatic red flag — see our
[scam warning list](./toast-wallet-scam-warning-list.md).

**"Deleting" a Toast Wallet account, technically:** because Toast Wallet is non-custodial, there
was never a central account to "delete" in the way you'd close a bank or exchange account. The app
itself can be uninstalled from your device at any time like any other app — this removes local app
data but has **no effect** on your XRP, which continues to exist on the XRP Ledger independently.
If you have funds in the wallet, move them out first (see our
[guide to moving XRP out](./how-to-move-xrp-out-of-toast-wallet.md)) before uninstalling,
since you'll lose convenient access to your locally-stored backup/credentials once the app is
gone (though a saved backup code elsewhere would still let you restore it later).

**If the app says "not working" or shows an error:** this is almost always one of two things —
either a [passphrase/recovery issue](./forgot-toast-wallet-passphrase.md), or the
[XRP Ledger reserve/"not activated" behavior](./toast-wallet-not-activated-20-xrp-reserve.md),
not a sign the software itself is broken or compromised.

**Where to actually get help today:** this site and the active XRP community (subreddits like
r/XRP and r/Ripple, where former users still occasionally help each other with recovery questions)
are the closest things to ongoing "support" available. There is no official paid support tier, and
anyone offering one is not affiliated with the real Toast Wallet.

**Fees:** Toast Wallet itself never charged fees. Any XRP transaction you send incurs only the
standard XRP Ledger network fee (a small fraction of a cent), paid to the network, not to Toast
Wallet or its original developers.

## ⚠️ Known Scams — Do Not Use These

- **The fake "Toast Plus" wallet app** — not affiliated with Toast Wallet, tied to over **$5M in stolen XRP** and a class-action lawsuit against Apple. See: [The Toast Plus App Scam](./toast-plus-app-scam.md).
- **The toastwallet.app phishing clone** — mimics the real recovery page to steal imported wallets. See: [Toast Wallet Phishing Sites](./toast-wallet-phishing-sites.md).
- **SEO clone sites impersonating Toast Wallet** — `xrp-toastwallet.com`, `toast-wallet.com`, `en-toastwallet.com`, `toast-wallet.net`.
- **Follow-up "recovery for a fee" scams** targeting people who already lost funds — never legitimate.

Complete list: [Toast Wallet Scam Alert: Full Warning List](./toast-wallet-scam-warning-list.md). To report a scam, visit [ic3.gov](https://www.ic3.gov).
