If you downloaded an app called **"Toast Plus"** from the Apple App Store or Google Play thinking
it was Toast Wallet, you may be a victim of one of the better-documented cryptocurrency wallet
scams of the last several years.

**What happened.** After the real Toast Wallet withdrew from app stores in mid-2020, a
near-identical app appeared using a similar name and a matching or nearly identical logo. On
Google Play it was listed under the package name `co.peninsulasoftware.toastwallet`. It ranked
high in search results for "XRP wallet" — meaning it was often *more* visible than the real thing
by the time people went looking for it.

**How the scam worked, per victim reports:** users would import an existing wallet or deposit XRP
into "Toast Plus." The app appeared to function normally at first. Then, without warning, the
account would be silently deleted — while the deposited XRP had already been routed out to
addresses controlled by the operator. Victims typically discovered the theft only when they came
back later to move their funds and found the account gone.

**The Apple lawsuit.** In September 2021, a cybersecurity professional named Hadona Diep filed a
federal class-action lawsuit against Apple (*Diep v. Apple Inc.*) alleging the company hosted and
failed to remove the fraudulent app. Diep had transferred 474 XRP into "Toast Plus" in March 2021;
by August 2021 her account had been deleted (backdated to March) and the funds were gone. The
complaint alleged **"hundreds or thousands" of victims and over $5 million in stolen XRP**. In
2022, Judge Phyllis J. Hamilton of the Northern District of California ruled Apple was shielded
from liability under Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act — the case against Apple was
dismissed, but the underlying fraud itself was never in dispute.

**How to check if this affected you:** if you ever deposited XRP into an app called "Toast Plus,"
"Toast Wallet+," or anything other than the exact official app built by StarStone Ltd, check your
transaction history on a public ledger explorer like bithomp.com or xrpscan.com using your wallet
address. If your funds were moved to an address you don't recognize around the time you used the
app, that's consistent with this scam pattern.

**What to do now:** stop using the app immediately, do not enter any further credentials into it,
and file a report at ic3.gov. Unfortunately, XRP Ledger transactions cannot be reversed — but
documenting the theft matters for any future enforcement action.

## ⚠️ Known Scams — Do Not Use These

- **The fake "Toast Plus" wallet app** — not affiliated with Toast Wallet, tied to over **$5M in stolen XRP** and a class-action lawsuit against Apple. See: [The Toast Plus App Scam](./toast-plus-app-scam.md).
- **The toastwallet.app phishing clone** — mimics the real recovery page to steal imported wallets. See: [Toast Wallet Phishing Sites](./toast-wallet-phishing-sites.md).
- **SEO clone sites impersonating Toast Wallet** — `xrp-toastwallet.com`, `toast-wallet.com`, `en-toastwallet.com`, `toast-wallet.net`.
- **Follow-up "recovery for a fee" scams** targeting people who already lost funds — never legitimate.

Complete list: [Toast Wallet Scam Alert: Full Warning List](./toast-wallet-scam-warning-list.md). To report a scam, visit [ic3.gov](https://www.ic3.gov).
