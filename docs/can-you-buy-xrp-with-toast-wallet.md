Short answer: **no.** Toast Wallet was never an exchange, and it never had a built-in way to
purchase XRP with a card, bank transfer, or any other payment method. This is a common
misunderstanding worth clearing up directly.

**What Toast Wallet actually is:** a *wallet*, not an exchange. It stores, sends, and receives XRP
that you already own — it doesn't sell it to you. This is the same relationship a physical wallet
has to cash: it holds your money, it doesn't print it.

**Where the confusion comes from:** some of the fraudulent clone sites impersonating Toast Wallet
(see our [scam warning list](./toast-wallet-scam-warning-list.md)) inaccurately describe it as
a full-featured "hosted" wallet with buying/selling built in — that was never true of the real
product, and any site claiming otherwise is not the official Toast Wallet.

**How people actually acquired XRP to put into Toast Wallet, historically:** by buying it first on
an exchange (Coinbase, Binance, Kraken, and others all support XRP purchases) and then withdrawing
it to their Toast Wallet address for storage — the reverse of the process described in our
[guide to moving XRP out to an exchange](./toast-wallet-to-coinbase-binance.md).

**If you're looking to buy XRP today:** you'd do so on a cryptocurrency exchange, then send it to
whichever wallet you're using for storage. Given that Toast Wallet is no longer actively
maintained, we'd recommend sending newly purchased XRP directly to an actively maintained wallet
like Xaman rather than into an old Toast Wallet — see our
[Toast Wallet alternatives roundup](./toast-wallet-alternatives.md) if you're deciding where to
hold XRP going forward.

**If you already have XRP sitting in an old Toast Wallet** and are trying to figure out what to do
with it — that's a different, much more common situation, and our
[recovery guide](./how-to-recover-xrp-toast-wallet.md) is the place to start.

## ⚠️ Known Scams — Do Not Use These

- **"Toast Plus"** — a fraudulent lookalike app that has stolen an estimated **$5M+ in XRP** and was the subject of a 2021 federal lawsuit against Apple. Read: [The Toast Plus App Scam](./toast-plus-app-scam.md).
- **toastwallet.app** — a phishing clone of the official recovery page; anything imported here gets stolen within minutes. Read: [Toast Wallet Phishing Sites](./toast-wallet-phishing-sites.md).
- **Clone marketing sites** (`xrp-toastwallet.com`, `toast-wallet.com`, `en-toastwallet.com`, `toast-wallet.net`) — unofficial, with fabricated claims about the product.
- **Anyone offering paid XRP "recovery" after you post about a loss** — this is always fraud.

Full reference: [Toast Wallet Scam Alert: Full Warning List](./toast-wallet-scam-warning-list.md). Report scams at [ic3.gov](https://www.ic3.gov).
