Since Toast Wallet is no longer actively maintained, most former users eventually move their XRP
to a wallet that is. Here's a quick overview of the main options, each covered in more depth
elsewhere on this site.

**Xaman (formerly XUMM) — best general-purpose replacement.** Actively maintained, mobile-first,
widely used across the XRP Ledger ecosystem, and specifically documents a migration path from
Toast Wallet. This is our default recommendation for most former Toast Wallet users. See our
[full migration guide](./migrate-toast-wallet-to-xaman.md).

**Gatehub — an alternative non-custodial option with more built-in features.** Supports XRP
alongside other assets and includes some trading functionality Toast Wallet never had. See our
[Toast Wallet vs. Gatehub comparison](./toast-wallet-vs-gatehub.md) for the specific tradeoffs.

**A hardware wallet (e.g. Ledger) — best for long-term cold storage.** If you're not planning to
transact frequently and want funds offline entirely, a hardware wallet is the more secure option
for larger holdings. See our
[Toast Wallet vs. Ledger Nano S comparison](./toast-wallet-vs-ledger-nano-s.md).

**An exchange wallet (Coinbase, Binance, etc.) — simplest, but custodial.** Convenient if you plan
to sell or trade soon, but you don't hold your own keys — the exchange does. Not recommended for
long-term storage of significant holdings. See our
[guide to sending XRP to an exchange](./toast-wallet-to-coinbase-binance.md).

**What we don't recommend:** continuing to use Toast Wallet itself as a long-term primary wallet.
Since it receives no security updates, any newly discovered vulnerability in the underlying
libraries it depends on would go unpatched. Use it to access and migrate existing funds, not to
hold new funds indefinitely.

**Before you move anything, make sure you're actually in your old wallet first** — see our
[full recovery guide](./how-to-recover-xrp-toast-wallet.md) if you haven't gotten that far yet.

## ⚠️ Known Scams — Do Not Use These

- **"Toast Plus"** — a fraudulent lookalike app that has stolen an estimated **$5M+ in XRP** and was the subject of a 2021 federal lawsuit against Apple. Read: [The Toast Plus App Scam](./toast-plus-app-scam.md).
- **toastwallet.app** — a phishing clone of the official recovery page; anything imported here gets stolen within minutes. Read: [Toast Wallet Phishing Sites](./toast-wallet-phishing-sites.md).
- **Clone marketing sites** (`xrp-toastwallet.com`, `toast-wallet.com`, `en-toastwallet.com`, `toast-wallet.net`) — unofficial, with fabricated claims about the product.
- **Anyone offering paid XRP "recovery" after you post about a loss** — this is always fraud.

Full reference: [Toast Wallet Scam Alert: Full Warning List](./toast-wallet-scam-warning-list.md). Report scams at [ic3.gov](https://www.ic3.gov).
