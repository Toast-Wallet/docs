Short answer: **the original Toast Wallet (built by StarStone Ltd, with source code on the
official GitHub) is safe and was well-regarded by the XRP community.**
The confusion — and the real danger — comes from apps and websites that borrowed its name.

**Why the real Toast Wallet earned trust:**
- Fully open-source — the code was public on GitHub for anyone to audit (`github.com/Toast-Wallet/core`).
- Non-custodial — it never held your private keys or funds on a server; everything was encrypted
  and stored locally on your device.
- No hidden monetization — the team was explicit that they ran on almost nothing (they once noted
  receiving about 0.07 XRP per user in donations) and never turned Toast Wallet into a paid or
  ad-supported product.
- A multi-layer credential system (PIN, passphrase, recovery phrase, and the underlying XRP secret
  key) designed to slow down anyone trying to brute-force access to your funds.

**Why it now shows up in scam warnings anyway:**
Not because the software itself was compromised, but because its abandoned brand became a target.
Since 2020, at least one fraudulent app ("Toast Plus") and one phishing website (`toastwallet.app`)
have used the name to steal funds from people searching for the real thing — see our
[full scam warning list](./toast-wallet-scam-warning-list.md) for the details, tracked
addresses, and legal case history.

**Is it still safe to use *today*?** With caveats. The underlying XRP Ledger integration still
works, and using the official desktop installer to recover an old wallet is safe.
But because the app hasn't been updated since 2020, there's no active security patching, and the
team itself recommends using it only to migrate funds out, not as a long-term wallet. Most
long-time users have moved to actively maintained wallets like Xaman (formerly XUMM) — see our
[migration guide](./migrate-toast-wallet-to-xaman.md).

**Bottom line:** the historical reputation is solid, the code was genuinely open and auditable,
and there's no credible evidence the original software itself was ever backdoored. The risk today
is entirely about brand impersonation, not the original product.

## ⚠️ Known Scams — Do Not Use These

- **"Toast Plus"** — a fraudulent lookalike app that has stolen an estimated **$5M+ in XRP** and was the subject of a 2021 federal lawsuit against Apple. Read: [The Toast Plus App Scam](./toast-plus-app-scam.md).
- **toastwallet.app** — a phishing clone of the official recovery page; anything imported here gets stolen within minutes. Read: [Toast Wallet Phishing Sites](./toast-wallet-phishing-sites.md).
- **Clone marketing sites** (`xrp-toastwallet.com`, `toast-wallet.com`, `en-toastwallet.com`, `toast-wallet.net`) — unofficial, with fabricated claims about the product.
- **Anyone offering paid XRP "recovery" after you post about a loss** — this is always fraud.

Full reference: [Toast Wallet Scam Alert: Full Warning List](./toast-wallet-scam-warning-list.md). Report scams at [ic3.gov](https://www.ic3.gov).
